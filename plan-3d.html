Main entrance as per Vastu (north-facing)

The main door faces north: it is on the house's north wall and you step out facing the road. Vastu divides the north side into 8 padas, counted from the north-west corner (N1) to the north-east corner (N8). N3 Mukhya, N4 Bhallata and N5 Soma are the auspicious ones. The door and the compound gate are both placed inside those three padas.
